# HoopTrack privacy policy

_Last updated: [date of publication]_

HoopTrack is a basketball training app. This policy explains what data it
handles and why. Questions: [contact email].

## Without an account

Everything stays on your phone:
- your sessions, shots, goals, badges and games;
- your session videos.

The camera is used only on the phone, to follow the ball and the rim. We
receive nothing.

## With an account (optional)

If you create an account, we store:
- your email address and an account ID, to sign you in;
- your sessions, shots, goals and badges, so you can back them up.

Your session videos are never uploaded.

## "Help improve shot tracking" (optional, off by default)

If you turn this on while signed in, a sample of still frames from your
session videos is uploaded after each session. It comes with a log of what
the shot detector saw and how each shot was scored. We use these only to
improve HoopTrack's shot detection. They may show anyone who was in view of
the camera, so only turn this on when the people in view are happy with it.

## Apple Health

If you allow it, HoopTrack saves your sessions to Apple Health as workouts.
It doesn't read your Health data, and nothing from Health is sent to us.

## Who processes the data

Data is stored with Supabase, our hosting provider, on our behalf. We don't
sell data. We don't use it for advertising. We don't share it with anyone
else or track you across apps or websites.

## Keeping and deleting data

Your data is kept until you delete it:
- **Profile → Delete account** removes your account and everything stored
  with it on our servers.
- **Profile → Delete all my data** also erases everything on the phone.
- **Profile → Export my data** gives you a copy at any time.

## Children

HoopTrack isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly
collect their data.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new
date.
